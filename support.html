Flight manual

Frequently asked

How do I move and mine?

Use the on-screen control to steer. In salvage sectors, hold the plasma button and draw a closed loop around a crystal, core, asteroid, or enemy. Then collect the loot and bring it back to the station.

Why did my own trail destroy me?

Fresh plasma is safe to cross. After it crystallizes, the trail becomes a solid hazard. Plan an exit before you close a large loop.

How do I keep the loot I collected?

Fly back to the station and bank it. Loot that is still aboard when a run ends may be lost, so choose when to turn back.

What is different about Battle chapters?

Battle runs focus on surviving a growing swarm. Steer your ship, collect crystals to level up, and choose modules. The ship's weapon targets enemies automatically.

Does the game work without internet?

You can play offline. The global leaderboard and rewarded ads need an internet connection. A score may be saved on your device and submitted when the connection returns.

Why did I not receive a rewarded-ad bonus?

The reward is granted after an ad finishes and the game receives its completion signal. If an ad fails to load or you close it early, try again later. The normal timed bonus remains available without watching an ad.

Can I change my callsign or remove my score?

Your callsign is used on the leaderboard. If you need help with a name or want your entry removed, email us with the callsign shown in the game. See the privacy policy for details.
