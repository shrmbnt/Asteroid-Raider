Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GSTD handles data in Asteroid Raider: Mine & Pilot, including the version displayed as CosmoBurst.io.

App: Asteroid Raider: Mine & Pilot (Google Play package com.aftertrace.game). Newer builds may display the name CosmoBurst.io.

Developer on Google Play: GSTD.

Privacy contact: goldstardevapp@gmail.com.

What We Store

  • Nickname: a nickname you choose at first launch (2-18 characters). It is stored locally on your device and submitted with your score to our leaderboard database in Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Anonymous device identifier: a UUID generated on your device. It is used only to prevent duplicate leaderboard entries from the same device.
  • Anonymous gameplay events: Google Analytics 4 may receive gameplay metrics such as run duration, score, coins earned, ship choice, and session length. No real-world contact information is attached to these events by GSTD.
  • Advertising data: when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google Mobile Ads may process an IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers where permitted by your device settings and consent choices. This is used to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

Data We Do Not Request for Gameplay

  • Email addresses, phone numbers, or real names (unless you choose to contact support)
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos, microphone, or camera data

Third Parties

  • Google Firebase Firestore: leaderboard storage
  • Google Analytics: aggregated gameplay metrics
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: optional rewarded ads, measurement, consent management, and fraud prevention

Rewarded Ads and Your Choices

Rewarded ads are optional. The game shows an explicit “Watch ad” choice, and declining or closing an ad does not remove your normal timed bonus. Where required, a Google consent form is shown before ads are requested. A Privacy Choices button is available in the game when regional rules require an ongoing consent-management entry point.

On iOS, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before Google Mobile Ads uses the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can decline or change this permission in iOS Settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. Depending on your consent and device settings, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.

Children

If you believe a child has provided data that should be removed, contact us using the address below.

Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard entries remain available while the leaderboard operates. Gameplay progress stored on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of your leaderboard entry, contact goldstardevapp@gmail.com. Include the nickname shown in the game so we can locate the entry.

If you contact us by email, we use your address and message to respond to your request. Google manages retention of data processed by Firebase, Analytics, and Mobile Ads under its own policies.

Security

The app uses encrypted HTTPS connections when sending data to Google services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
