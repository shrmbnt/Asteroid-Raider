GSTD / Privacy policy

Your data.
Your choices.

This policy explains what data CosmoBurst.io uses for gameplay, leaderboards, analytics, and optional rewarded ads.

App & developer

App: CosmoBurst.io, also listed on Google Play as Asteroid Raider: Mine & Pilot (package com.aftertrace.game). These names refer to the same app.

Developer named on Google Play: GSTD.

Privacy contact: goldstardevapp@gmail.com.

01 / Data

What the app stores or processes

  • Callsign and scores: the nickname you choose is stored on your device and submitted with leaderboard results to Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Player identifier: an identifier generated for your player profile helps maintain leaderboard entries and avoid duplicates. Firebase may also use an anonymous authentication identifier.
  • Gameplay analytics: Google Analytics may receive events such as run duration, score, coins earned, ship choice, and session length. GSTD does not attach your email address or real name to these events.
  • Advertising data: when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers where permitted by your settings and consent choices. This supports ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

02 / Boundaries

Data we do not ask for in gameplay

CosmoBurst.io does not ask you to provide a real name, email address, or phone number to play. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location. If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send.

03 / Partners

Services used by the app

  • Google Firebase Firestore and anonymous authentication: leaderboard storage and player identity for scores.
  • Google Analytics: gameplay and performance metrics.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: optional rewarded ads, consent management, measurement, and fraud prevention.

04 / Control

Rewarded ads & choices

Rewarded ads are optional. The game offers a clear choice to watch an ad; declining or closing one does not remove the normal timed bonus. Where required, a Google consent form appears before ads are requested. The in-game Privacy Choices control is available when regional rules require it.

On iOS, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before Google Mobile Ads uses the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can decline or change this in iOS Settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. Depending on consent and device settings, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.

05 / Requests

Retention & deletion

Leaderboard entries remain available while the leaderboard operates. Gameplay progress stored on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. To request deletion of a leaderboard entry, email goldstardevapp@gmail.com and include the callsign shown in the game so we can locate it.

When you contact support, we use your email address and message to respond. Google manages retention of data processed by Firebase, Analytics, and Mobile Ads under its own policies.

06 / Protection

Security & children

The app uses encrypted HTTPS connections when sending data to Google services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

If you believe a child has provided data that should be removed, contact us using the address above.
